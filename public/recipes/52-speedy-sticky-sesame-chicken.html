Speedy sticky sesame chicken

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts (bite-sized pieces)
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tsp garlic paste
  • 2 tsp ginger paste
  • 80ml chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1½ tbsp toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 1 tbsp cold water
  • toasted sesame seeds
  • sliced spring onions
