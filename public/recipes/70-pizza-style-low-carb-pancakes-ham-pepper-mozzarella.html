Pizza-style low-carb pancakes (ham, pepper & mozzarella)

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 60g ham
  • 50g pepper (bell)
  • 30g almond flour
  • 60g mozzarella

Method

  1. Mix eggs with almond flour.
  2. Stir in finely chopped ham, pepper and grated mozzarella.
  3. Cook in a pancake pan for a few minutes until golden.
