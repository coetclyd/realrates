Crispy potato, salami & egg breakfast

Ingredients

  • 1 potato
  • kashar (or mozzarella) cheese
  • salami or smoked meat
  • 1 egg

Method

  1. Cook the sliced/grated potato in a little oil (as in the video), crack an egg on top and fill with cheese and salami.
