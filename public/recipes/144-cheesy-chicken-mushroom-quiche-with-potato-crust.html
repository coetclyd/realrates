Cheesy chicken & mushroom quiche with potato crust

Ingredients

  • 4 medium Yukon Gold potatoes (~500g, boiled, peeled)
  • 30g grated parmesan
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 350g chicken thighs
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • 200g chestnut mushrooms
  • 1 onion
  • 3 large eggs
  • 60g sour cream
  • 120ml whole milk
  • 2 spring onions
  • 100g mozzarella in strips + 60g grated
  • basil
