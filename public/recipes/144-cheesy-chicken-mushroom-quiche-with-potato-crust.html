Cheesy chicken & mushroom quiche with potato crust

Ingredients

  • 4 medium Yukon Gold potatoes (~500g, boiled, peeled)
  • 30g grated parmesan
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 350g chicken thighs
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • 200g chestnut mushrooms
  • 1 onion
  • 3 large eggs
  • 60g sour cream
  • 120ml whole milk
  • 2 spring onions
  • 100g mozzarella in strips + 60g grated
  • basil

Method

  1. Boil potatoes ~20 min, steam-dry 3 min.
  2. Press into a lined 20cm springform tin up the sides with a glass; sprinkle parmesan.
  3. Season chicken and fry 5–6 min per side until cooked; slice.
  4. Fry mushrooms 4 min, add onion 3 min.
  5. Whisk eggs, sour cream, milk and seasoning; fold in chicken, veg, spring onion and mozzarella strips.
  6. Pour into crust, top with grated mozzarella.
  7. Bake 190°C for 35–40 min until set.
  8. Rest 10 min, garnish with basil.
