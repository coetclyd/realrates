Cheesy courgette lasagne

Ingredients

  • 1 medium courgette (approx. 200g)
  • 100g cooked ham
  • 80g mozzarella
  • 20g parmesan
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • fresh basil or oregano (optional)

Method

  1. Thinly slice the courgette lengthways and pat dry with kitchen paper.
  2. Heat the oven or air fryer to 180°C and lightly oil a small baking dish.
  3. Lay a single layer of courgette in the dish, scatter over ham, then mozzarella and a little parmesan.
  4. Repeat the layers until the dish is full, finishing with plenty of cheese.
  5. Drizzle with the olive oil.
  6. Bake for 20–25 minutes until bubbling and golden.
  7. Rest 3–5 minutes, then serve with basil or oregano.
