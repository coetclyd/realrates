Cheesy potato flatbreads (pan-fried)

Ingredients

  • 4 small–medium potatoes
  • 1 egg
  • 200g kashar (or mozzarella) cheese
  • parsley
  • chilli flakes
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1 cup flour
  • butter for brushing

Method

  1. Peel and boil potatoes in a little salted water until soft; drain and mash.
  2. Mix with everything else into a dough.
  3. Divide into balls, roll out flat and dry-fry in a hot pan on both sides.
  4. Brush with butter when done.
