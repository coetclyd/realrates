Slow cooker cheesy beef & potato casserole

Ingredients

  • 900g (2 lb) beef mince
  • 4–5 large potatoes (peeled, 6mm slices)
  • 1 large onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tins (~300g each) condensed cream of mushroom or chicken soup
  • 1 cup beef stock
  • 2 cups grated mature cheddar
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • parsley

Method

  1. Brown mince and onion in oil; drain; add garlic 1 min.
  2. Whisk soup, stock and seasonings.
  3. Grease slow cooker; layer half the potatoes, the beef, then the rest of the potatoes.
  4. Pour the sauce over.
  5. Cook LOW 6–8 hrs or HIGH 3–4 hrs.
  6. Add cheese for the last 20–30 min to melt.
  7. Garnish with parsley.
