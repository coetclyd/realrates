Crispy smashed Jersey Royal quiche (peas, feta & mint)

Ingredients

  • 500g Jersey Royal potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp grated parmesan
  • 4 large eggs
  • 120ml double cream
  • 80g mature cheddar or Gruyère
  • 150g frozen peas
  • 100g feta
  • 2 tbsp chopped mint
  • extra feta
  • pea shoots
  • lemon zest. 20cm tart tin

Method

  1. Boil potatoes 15 min, drain.
  2. Oven 200°C fan.
  3. Put potatoes in greased tin and squash with a glass into a crust; drizzle oil, season, sprinkle parmesan.
  4. Bake 20 min till golden.
  5. Whisk eggs, cream & cheese; stir in peas, feta & mint.
  6. Pour into crust, bake 18–22 min till just set.
  7. Rest 10 min, finish with feta, pea shoots, lemon zest.
