Haggis & hot honey halloumi flatbreads

Ingredients

  • 300g haggis
  • 4 slices halloumi
  • 75g rocket
  • 4–5 chopped pickled chillies
  • 3–4 tbsp honey
  • 2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tomato
  • ½ red onion
  • 1–2 tsp crème fraîche per wrap

Method

  1. Fry haggis on high 5 min until crispy; remove.
  2. Fry halloumi until golden on one side, flip, add honey and chilli flakes and coat.
  3. Slice tomato, onion & chillies.
  4. Spread flatbreads with crème fraîche, add rocket, tomato, onion, then haggis, halloumi & chillies.
  5. Drizzle with the leftover hot honey.
