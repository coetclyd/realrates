Haggis & hot honey halloumi flatbreads

Ingredients

  • 300g haggis
  • 4 slices halloumi
  • 75g rocket
  • 4–5 chopped pickled chillies
  • 3–4 tbsp honey
  • 2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tomato
  • ½ red onion
  • 1–2 tsp crème fraîche per wrap
