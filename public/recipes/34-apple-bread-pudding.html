Apple bread pudding

Ingredients

  • 3 medium eggs
  • 120ml milk
  • 1 tsp mixed spice
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • 80g white bread
  • 100g apple (1 medium)
  • cinnamon
  • honey

Method

  1. Mix eggs, milk, spice/cinnamon & baking powder with a fork in an ovenproof dish.
  2. Add chopped bread and press down.
  3. Top with sliced apple and a drizzle of honey, sprinkle cinnamon.
  4. Bake 200°C for 30 min (watch the apple doesn't burn).
