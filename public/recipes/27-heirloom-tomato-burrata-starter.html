Heirloom tomato & burrata starter

Ingredients

  • 2–3 heirloom tomatoes
  • ¾–1 cup olive oil
  • balsamic glaze
  • 2–4 burrata
  • handful of basil
  • ½ cup pesto
  • Italian loaf toasted in olive oil

Method

  1. Layer sliced tomatoes and burrata on a plate, salting each layer; drizzle with oil, pesto and balsamic glaze, scatter basil.
  2. Serve with the toasted bread.
