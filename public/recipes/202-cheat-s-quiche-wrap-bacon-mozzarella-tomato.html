Cheat's quiche wrap (bacon, mozzarella & tomato)

Ingredients

  • 1 large high-protein wrap
  • 2 rashers back bacon
  • 6 cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 60g mozzarella, torn
  • 3 medium eggs
  • fresh basil
  • sriracha (optional)

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C fan.
  2. Fry or grill the bacon until just crisp and chop it.
  3. Press the wrap into a small ovenproof dish or tin to make a case.
  4. Scatter in the bacon, tomatoes and mozzarella.
  5. Whisk the eggs and pour them over.
  6. Bake 15–18 min until set and the wrap edges are crisp.
  7. Top with basil and a drizzle of sriracha if you like.
