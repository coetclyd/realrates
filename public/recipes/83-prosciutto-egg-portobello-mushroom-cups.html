Prosciutto & egg portobello mushroom cups

Ingredients

  • large portobello mushrooms
  • eggs
  • prosciutto
  • everything-bagel seasoning
  • chives

Method

  1. Remove mushroom stems, line each with prosciutto, crack in an egg, sprinkle seasoning.
  2. Bake until the egg is set and prosciutto crispy.
  3. Top with chives.
