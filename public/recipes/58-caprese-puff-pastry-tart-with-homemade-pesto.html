Caprese puff pastry tart with homemade pesto

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 3 tbsp pesto
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp pine nuts
  • 2 balls fresh mozzarella
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • fresh basil
  • 80g basil
  • 2 tbsp toasted pine nuts
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 70g Romano or parmesan
  • 100ml extra virgin olive oil
  • ½ tsp coarse salt

Method

  1. Pesto – pound garlic & salt to a paste, add pine nuts, then basil in batches, stir in cheese and slowly add oil.
  2. Tart – spread pesto on the pastry, layer tomato & mozzarella slices, add pine nuts and oil.
  3. Bake 20–25 min until golden and crisp; top with basil.
