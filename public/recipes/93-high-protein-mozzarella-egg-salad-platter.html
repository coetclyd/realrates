High-protein mozzarella & egg salad platter

Ingredients

  • 3 hard-boiled eggs (halved)
  • ½ cup mini mozzarella balls
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes (halved)
  • ½ cup sweetcorn
  • 1 cucumber (sliced)
  • crisp lettuce
  • extra virgin olive oil or light dressing

Method

  1. Arrange everything in sections on a big plate, drizzle with oil, season well and toss just before eating.
