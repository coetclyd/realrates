Crispy chicken alfredo wraps

Ingredients

  • 450g chicken breast (small pieces)
  • salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp Italian seasoning
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp oil + more for cooking
  • 1 onion (diced)
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • ¾ cup double cream
  • 1 cup milk
  • salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • ½ cup grated parmesan
  • 1 tsp parsley
  • 6–7 tortillas
  • 1 cup grated mozzarella
  • oil

Method

  1. Marinate chicken in the seasonings, lemon and oil 20–30 min.
  2. Fry 5–7 min until cooked, add onion for 1–2 min; set aside.
  3. Sauce: melt butter, fry garlic 30 sec, whisk in flour 30–45 sec, then slowly the cream and milk; season and simmer 2–4 min, stir in parmesan and parsley.
  4. Toss chicken in half the sauce.
  5. Fill tortillas with chicken and mozzarella, roll tightly.
  6. Fry seam-side down 2–3 min per side until golden.
  7. Serve with the rest of the sauce for dipping.
