Camembert & meatball puff pastry wreath

Ingredients

  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 1 camembert
  • ~20 small beef meatballs
  • 1 egg yolk + splash of water
  • sesame seeds
  • 1 garlic clove
  • thyme
  • 1 tsp white wine

Method

  1. Assemble the camembert (studded with garlic, thyme and wine) in the middle of the pastry with meatballs wrapped around it in a lattice (see video).
  2. Brush with egg wash, add sesame seeds, bake 40–45 min at 180°C.
