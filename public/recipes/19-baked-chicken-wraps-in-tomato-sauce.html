Baked chicken wraps in tomato sauce

Ingredients

  • 5 tortillas/lavash
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 3 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 bowl tinned tomato sauce/passata
  • 300g chicken breast
  • 3 red peppers
  • 2 onions
  • chilli flakes
  • ~50ml oil
  • 2 portions of chips/fried potatoes
  • 1 bowl grated cheese

Method

  1. Dice chicken; slice peppers & onions.
  2. Fry peppers & onions in oil, add chicken and cook through, then season.
  3. Fill each wrap with chicken, chips & cheese and roll up.
  4. Mix the sauce and pour over the wraps.
  5. Bake at 200°C for 15 min.
