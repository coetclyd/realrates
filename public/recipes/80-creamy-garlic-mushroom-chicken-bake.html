Creamy garlic mushroom chicken bake

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 280g sliced mushrooms
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 cup double cream
  • ½ cup chicken stock
  • ½ cup grated parmesan
  • ½ tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1½ cups grated mozzarella
  • parsley

Method

  1. Oven 190°C.
  2. Season chicken and sear in oil until browned; put in a baking dish.
  3. Melt butter in the pan, fry mushrooms till golden, add garlic 30 sec.
  4. Add stock, cream, parmesan & seasoning; simmer until thickened.
  5. Pour over the chicken, cover with mozzarella.
  6. Bake 25–30 min until cooked through.
  7. Top with parsley.
