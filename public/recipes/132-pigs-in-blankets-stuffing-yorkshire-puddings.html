Pigs in blankets & stuffing Yorkshire puddings

Ingredients

  • 8 pigs in blankets
  • 1 packet sage & onion stuffing mix
  • mashed potato and gravy to serve
  • dried parsley (optional)

Method

  1. Oven 200°C (180°C fan).
  2. Cook pigs in blankets 20–25 min.
  3. Make the stuffing per the packet.
  4. Fill each Yorkshire with stuffing and 2 pigs in blankets; return to the oven 5–8 min until crisp and hot.
  5. Serve with mash, lots of gravy and a sprinkle of parsley.
