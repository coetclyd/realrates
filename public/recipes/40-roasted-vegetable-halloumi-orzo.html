Roasted vegetable halloumi orzo

Ingredients

  • 150g halloumi (cubed)
  • 1 courgette
  • 1 red onion
  • 300g cherry tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 50g parmesan
  • fresh basil

Method

  1. Oven 200°C.
  2. Toss courgette, onion & tomatoes with 1 tbsp oil and oregano; roast 25 min.
  3. Cook orzo in salted water, drain.
  4. Fry halloumi in remaining oil till golden, add garlic for 1 min.
  5. Mix veg, orzo, halloumi & parmesan; finish with basil.
