Stuffed mushrooms (veg, egg & mozzarella)

Ingredients

  • large mushrooms
  • courgette
  • tomato
  • pepper
  • spring onions
  • eggs
  • mozzarella
  • salt & pepper
  • olive oil

Method

  1. Fill the mushrooms with the chopped veg, egg and mozzarella, drizzle with oil and bake about 15 min at 200°C.
