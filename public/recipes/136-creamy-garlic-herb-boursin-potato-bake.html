Creamy garlic & herb Boursin potato bake

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 150g Boursin garlic & herb
  • 1 cup grated mozzarella
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1 cup veg or chicken stock
  • ¼ cup plain flour
  • 1 small onion (sliced)
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ¼ tsp pepper
  • 2 tbsp chives

Method

  1. Oven 190°C; grease a 9x13in dish.
  2. Soften onion in butter 2–3 min, whisk in flour 1 min, then slowly the milk until thick.
  3. Add stock, simmer, then whisk in Boursin, salt and pepper.
  4. Layer half the potatoes, half the sauce, repeat.
  5. Top with mozzarella.
  6. Cover with foil and bake 30 min, uncover and bake 35–40 min more until tender and golden.
  7. Rest 15 min, top with chives.
