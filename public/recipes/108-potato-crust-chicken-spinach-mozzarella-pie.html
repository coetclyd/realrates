Potato-crust chicken, spinach & mozzarella pie

Ingredients

  • 250g chicken breast
  • 5 eggs
  • 1 tbsp cream cheese
  • 150g mozzarella
  • 40g baby spinach
  • 20–30g parmesan
  • olive oil
  • paprika
  • dried garlic
  • chilli
  • ground coriander

Method

  1. Boil potatoes in skins, peel and press into a greased tin to form base and sides.
  2. Drizzle oil, add spices and parmesan; bake 200°C for 12–15 min.
  3. Dice, season and quickly fry the chicken.
  4. Mix eggs with cream cheese, add chicken, spinach and diced mozzarella.
  5. Pour into the crust, top with parmesan, bake 180°C for 25–30 min until golden.
