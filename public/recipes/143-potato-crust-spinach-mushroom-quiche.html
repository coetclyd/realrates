Potato-crust spinach & mushroom quiche

Ingredients

  • 700g baby potatoes (boiled, halved)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 leek (sliced)
  • 225g mushrooms (sliced)
  • 3 big handfuls baby spinach
  • 6 large eggs
  • ¾ cup (180ml) milk
  • 1 cup grated cheddar or Gruyère
  • salt
  • pepper

Method

  1. Oven 190°C; grease a baking dish.
  2. Spread potatoes in it and smash flat with a glass to make a crust.
  3. Fry leek 3 min, add mushrooms and a pinch of salt until golden; stir in spinach to wilt.
  4. Spread veg over the crust.
  5. Whisk eggs, milk and seasoning; pour over.
  6. Top with cheese.
  7. Bake 30–35 min until set and golden.
  8. Rest 10 min.
