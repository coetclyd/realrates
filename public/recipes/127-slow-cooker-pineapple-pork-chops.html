Slow cooker pineapple pork chops

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless pork chops (about 2.5cm thick)
  • 1 tin (about 435–560g) pineapple chunks in juice, not drained
  • 50g light brown sugar
  • 60ml reduced-salt soy sauce
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp grated fresh ginger (optional)
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp cornflour mixed with 2 tbsp cold water
  • 2 spring onions, sliced, to serve

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a frying pan and brown the chops for 2 min each side, then put them in the slow cooker.
  2. Mix the pineapple and its juice with the sugar, soy sauce, garlic and ginger, and pour it over the chops.
  3. Cook on low for 6–7 hours or high for 3–4 hours until the pork is tender.
  4. Lift the chops out onto a plate and keep warm.
  5. Stir the cornflour mix into the sauce, turn to high and cook 15 min until thickened.
  6. Spoon the sauce and pineapple over the chops and top with spring onions.
  7. Serve with rice.
