Low-carb cabbage waffle burger

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • garlic powder
  • ½ cup grated mozzarella
  • Fillings: burger patty
  • lettuce
  • tomato
  • cheese slice
  • pickles
  • sauces

Method

  1. Lightly steam/blanch the cabbage until tender, drain and cool.
  2. Mix with eggs, seasoning and mozzarella.
  3. Cook scoops in a greased waffle maker until golden and crisp.
  4. Sandwich your patty and fillings between two cabbage waffles.
