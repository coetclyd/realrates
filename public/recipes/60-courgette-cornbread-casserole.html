Courgette cornbread casserole

Ingredients

  • 2 medium courgettes grated (~3 cups)
  • ½ sweet onion chopped
  • 1 cup sweetcorn
  • 450g (1 lb) grated cheddar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 jalapeño (seeded, diced)
  • ¼ tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 box (8.5oz/240g) Jiffy cornbread mix

Method

  1. Mix courgette, onion, corn, half the cheese, eggs, jalapeño and spices in a big bowl.
  2. Stir in the cornbread mix.
  3. Pour into a greased 9x9in (23cm) dish, top with the rest of the cheese.
  4. Bake 180°C (350°F) for 45–50 min.
