Courgette cornbread casserole

Ingredients

  • 2 medium courgettes, grated (about 3 cups)
  • ½ sweet onion, chopped
  • 1 cup sweetcorn
  • 450g grated cheddar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 jalapeño (seeded, diced)
  • ¼ tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 75g fine polenta or cornmeal
  • 75g plain flour
  • 2 tbsp caster sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 40g butter, melted

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan) and grease a 23cm square dish.
  2. In a small bowl mix the polenta, flour, sugar and baking powder, then stir in the melted butter until it looks like damp crumbs.
  3. In a big bowl mix the courgette, onion, sweetcorn, half the cheddar, the eggs, jalapeño, garlic powder and cumin.
  4. Stir in the polenta mix until everything is combined.
  5. Pour into the dish and top with the rest of the cheddar.
  6. Bake 45–50 min until golden and set in the middle.
  7. Rest 10 min before cutting.
