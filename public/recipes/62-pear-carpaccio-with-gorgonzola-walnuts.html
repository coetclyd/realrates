Pear carpaccio with gorgonzola & walnuts

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe pear
  • handful of salad leaves
  • 50g gorgonzola
  • 2 tbsp balsamic
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • walnuts

Method

  1. Thinly slice the pear onto a plate, top with leaves, crumbled gorgonzola and walnuts; whisk balsamic, honey and oil and drizzle over.
