Boursin sausage pasta

Ingredients

  • 6 sausages
  • 150g dried pasta
  • 1 red onion
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 35g parmesan
  • 50ml single cream
  • 50g Boursin
  • fresh parsley
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 200ml veg stock
  • 1 tsp each garlic granules
  • onion granules
  • Italian seasoning
  • oregano + pinch chilli flakes

Method

  1. Fry onion a few mins, skin the sausages into the pan and break up, add seasoning.
  2. Add garlic & purée, cook 1–2 mins, then stock, cream & Boursin; simmer.
  3. Cook pasta; 5 mins before the end add parmesan, peas & parsley to the sauce.
  4. Stir pasta through, top with chilli flakes.
