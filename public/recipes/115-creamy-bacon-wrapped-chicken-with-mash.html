Creamy bacon-wrapped chicken with mash

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breast mini fillets
  • 4 thin rashers streaky bacon
  • oil
  • butter
  • cherry tomatoes (halved)
  • dry seasoning
  • cooking cream
  • a spoonful of thick cream (crème fraîche)
  • baby spinach
  • parmesan
  • ~12 baby potatoes
  • milk or cream
  • nutmeg

Method

  1. Wrap each chicken fillet in bacon and sear in a little oil until the bacon is browned; remove.
  2. Melt butter in the pan, add tomatoes and seasoning and cook until softening.
  3. Add cooking cream and a spoonful of thick cream, then spinach and grated parmesan until wilted and melted.
  4. Return chicken and coat in sauce.
  5. Boil peeled baby potatoes, drain and mash with butter, milk, salt and nutmeg.
  6. Serve chicken and sauce on the mash.
