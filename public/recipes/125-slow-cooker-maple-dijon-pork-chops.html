Slow cooker maple Dijon pork chops

Ingredients

  • ½ cup maple syrup
  • ¼ cup Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce

Method

  1. Lightly grease the slow cooker and lay the chops in.
  2. Whisk maple, Dijon and soy and pour over.
  3. Cook on LOW 4–6 hrs until tender (63°C inside).
  4. Spoon sauce over to serve; good with mash, veg, rice or crusty bread.
