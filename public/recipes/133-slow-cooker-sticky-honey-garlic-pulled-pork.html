Slow cooker sticky honey garlic pulled pork

Ingredients

  • 1.6–1.8kg (3½–4 lb) pork shoulder
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large onion (sliced)
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • ½ cup low-salt soy sauce
  • ½ cup honey
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • ¼ cup balsamic vinegar
  • ¼ cup ketchup
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • 2 tbsp cornflour + 3 tbsp cold water
  • parsley
