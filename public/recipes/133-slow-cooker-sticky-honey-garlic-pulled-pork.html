Slow cooker sticky honey garlic pulled pork

Ingredients

  • 1.6–1.8kg (3½–4 lb) pork shoulder
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large onion (sliced)
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • ½ cup low-salt soy sauce
  • ½ cup honey
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • ¼ cup balsamic vinegar
  • ¼ cup ketchup
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • 2 tbsp cornflour + 3 tbsp cold water
  • parsley

Method

  1. Pat pork dry and rub with salt, pepper, paprika and onion powder.
  2. Sear in oil 3–4 min per side; put in slow cooker with onion and garlic around it.
  3. Whisk soy, honey, sugar, balsamic, ketchup and Worcestershire and pour over.
  4. Cook LOW 8–9 hrs or HIGH 5–6 hrs.
  5. Shred and return to the pot.
  6. Stir in the cornflour slurry and cook on HIGH 15–20 min until thick.
  7. Optional: grill on a tray 2–3 min to caramelise.
  8. Garnish with parsley.
