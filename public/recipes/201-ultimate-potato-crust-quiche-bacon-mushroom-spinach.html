Ultimate potato-crust quiche (bacon, mushroom & spinach)

Ingredients

  • 700g baby potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 30g grated parmesan
  • 4 rashers smoked back bacon, chopped
  • 200g chestnut mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 small onion, finely sliced
  • 2 handfuls baby spinach
  • 6 large eggs
  • 120ml whole milk
  • 2 tbsp crème fraîche
  • 100g mature cheddar, grated
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • chives to finish (optional)

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes for 15 min until tender, drain and steam-dry for 3 min.
  2. Heat the oven to 200°C fan and grease a 20–23cm springform tin.
  3. Press the potatoes into the tin and up the sides with an oiled glass to make a crust.
  4. Drizzle with oil, sprinkle with parmesan and bake for 20 min until golden and crisp.
  5. Meanwhile fry the bacon until crisp, add the onion and mushrooms and cook 5 min until golden, then stir in the spinach to wilt.
  6. Whisk the eggs, milk and crème fraîche, then stir in most of the cheddar, the spring onions and the bacon mix.
  7. Pour into the crust, top with the rest of the cheddar and turn the oven down to 180°C fan.
  8. Bake 30–35 min until just set with a slight wobble.
  9. Rest 10 min, unmould and finish with chives.
