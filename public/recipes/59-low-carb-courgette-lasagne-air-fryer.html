Low-carb courgette lasagne (air fryer)

Ingredients

  • 100g parmesan
  • 150g cooked ham (or turkey ham)
  • 250g cream cheese
  • diced mozzarella for the top

Method

  1. Thinly slice courgettes with a mandoline/peeler and rest 10–15 min wrapped in baking paper.
  2. Layer in the air fryer basket/tray: courgette, parmesan, ham, cream cheese; repeat for at least 4 layers and press down well.
  3. Top with parmesan and diced mozzarella.
  4. Air fry 170°C for 35–40 min.
  5. Rest a few mins before cutting.
