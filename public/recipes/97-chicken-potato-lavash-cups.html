Chicken & potato lavash cups

Ingredients

  • 4–6 lavash/tortilla wraps
  • 1 tbsp flour + a little water
  • 3–4 potatoes
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • splash of milk
  • 400–500g chicken breast
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 green pepper
  • 1 small tomato
  • 1–2 tbsp oil
  • paprika
  • dried thyme (optional)
  • 150–200g grated cheese

Method

  1. Make the mash.
  2. Dice and fry the chicken with the chopped onion, pepper and tomato; season.
  3. Brush wraps with the flour-water paste, snip two opposite sides and overlap the edges to form cups (in a tin).
  4. Fill: mash → chicken → mash → cheese.
  5. Bake 180°C for 25–30 min until golden.
