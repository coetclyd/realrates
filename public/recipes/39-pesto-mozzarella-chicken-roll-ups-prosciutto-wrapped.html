Pesto mozzarella chicken roll-ups (prosciutto-wrapped)

Ingredients

  • 1 small chicken breast halved and flattened (~110g each)
  • 2 tsp pesto
  • 4 sundried tomatoes
  • 2 mozzarella string cheese sticks (halved)
  • fresh basil
  • mixed salad & dressing to serve

Method

  1. Lay 5 prosciutto slices overlapping and put a flattened chicken piece in the middle.
  2. Spread with 1 tsp pesto; on the bottom half layer 2 sundried tomatoes, a halved cheese stick and basil; add pepper.
  3. Roll up tightly in the prosciutto.
  4. Repeat.
  5. Air fry at 180°C for 15 min until cooked through and crispy.
  6. Serve with salad.
