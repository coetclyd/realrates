Halloumi & chickpea bake

Ingredients

  • 400g tin chickpeas (drained)
  • 1 cup passata
  • 300g cherry tomatoes (halved)
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • fresh parsley

Method

  1. Soften the garlic in the oil.
  2. Stir in chickpeas, passata, tomatoes and paprika; simmer 5 min.
  3. Tip into a baking dish, top with halloumi and a drizzle of oil.
  4. Bake 190°C for 20–25 min until bubbling and golden.
  5. Season and finish with parsley.
