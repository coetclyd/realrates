Crispy potato & cheese omelette

Ingredients

  • 1 potato
  • 1 egg
  • kashar (or mozzarella/cheddar) cheese
  • chilli flakes
  • oil

Method

  1. Heat oil in a non-stick pan.
  2. Peel and finely chop the potato and fry until crisp.
  3. Pour the beaten egg over, season, add cheese, fold in half and fry both sides.
  4. Serve hot.
