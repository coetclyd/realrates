Ham, cheese & tomato egg tortilla

Ingredients

  • 1 tortilla
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 slice ham
  • ~100g cheese (50g for the pan + 50g for the middle)
  • 4 tomato slices
  • chives
  • butter

Method

  1. Melt butter in a pan, sprinkle in cheese and lay tomato slices on it.
  2. Mix eggs, chopped ham & chives, season and pour over the tomatoes.
  3. Put the tortilla on top.
  4. Fry on low heat and flip once the egg is set.
  5. Sprinkle with cheese, fold in half and toast both sides.
