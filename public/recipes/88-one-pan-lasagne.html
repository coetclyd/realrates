One-pan lasagne

Ingredients

  • 10 lasagne sheets
  • 250g beef mince
  • 1 onion
  • 2 green peppers
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 tomato (grated)
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 3½ glasses (~700ml) water
  • parsley

Method

  1. Brown the mince, add the diced veg and garlic and cook until soft; stir in tomato purée.
  2. Add grated tomato, broken lasagne sheets and the water, season, and simmer 15–20 min until the pasta is cooked.
