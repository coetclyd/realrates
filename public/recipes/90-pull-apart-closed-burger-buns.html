Pull-apart "closed" burger buns

Ingredients

  • 1 cup warm milk
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 5g instant yeast
  • 1 egg (yolk kept for brushing)
  • 4–4½ cups flour
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 tsp paprika/chilli
  • 2 large onions
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • cheddar
  • pickles
  • chilli flakes
  • 4 tbsp mayo
  • 2 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tbsp mustard
  • 2 finely chopped gherkins

Method

  1. Mix milk, water, sugar & yeast, rest 5 min.
  2. Add egg white, salt & flour, knead; add butter, knead 5 min more.
  3. Rise ~1 hr.
  4. Slowly cook onions in oil with salt & sugar ~15 min.
  5. Season mince and shape 8 patties.
  6. Flatten dough balls into a tin, top each with a patty, onions, chilli, pickles and cheddar, and enclose.
  7. Brush with yolk.
  8. Bake 180°C for 20–25 min until golden.
  9. Serve with the sauce.
