Creamy salmon & spinach orzo "dump dinner"

Ingredients

  • 240g orzo
  • 2 salmon fillets
  • 3 handfuls spinach
  • 400ml chicken stock
  • 150ml double cream
  • 80g grated parmesan
  • juice of ½ lemon
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning

Method

  1. Oven 190°C fan.
  2. Mix everything except the salmon in an ovenproof dish.
  3. Press salmon in so it's half submerged and spoon some liquid over.
  4. Cover with foil, bake 35 min.
  5. Flake the salmon and stir through.
  6. Serve with broccoli.
