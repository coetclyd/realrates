Creamy salmon & spinach orzo "dump dinner"

Ingredients

  • 240g orzo
  • 2 salmon fillets
  • 3 handfuls spinach
  • 400ml chicken stock
  • 150ml double cream
  • 80g grated parmesan
  • juice of ½ lemon
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
