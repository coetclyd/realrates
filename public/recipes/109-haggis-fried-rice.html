Haggis fried rice

Ingredients

  • 1 pack bacon lardons
  • 150–200g day-old cooked rice
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 spring onions (sliced)
  • sesame oil
  • soy sauce
  • curry sauce

Method

  1. Heat a wok very hot and fry the bacon; cook the haggis (microwave) until crispy.
  2. Remove bacon.
  3. Fry the rice a few mins with sesame oil and soy to taste.
  4. Push rice aside, scramble the beaten eggs, then mix in.
  5. Add bacon, haggis and spring onion.
  6. Top with more spring onion (and curry sauce if you like).
