Cabbage steaks topped with mince & cheese

Ingredients

  • 1 young/pointed cabbage
  • 400–500g mince
  • 150–200g cheese
  • sour cream or mayo
  • cherry tomatoes
  • oil
  • favourite spices

Method

  1. Cut cabbage into 2cm steaks and lay on oiled baking paper; brush with oil and season.
  2. Spread a thin layer of sour cream/mayo on top.
  3. Season the mince and press onto each steak.
  4. Add halved cherry tomatoes and grated cheese.
  5. Bake at 180°C for about 50 min until golden.
