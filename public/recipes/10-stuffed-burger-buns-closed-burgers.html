Stuffed burger buns (closed burgers)

Ingredients

  • 1 cup warm milk
  • ~100ml warm water
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 5g instant yeast
  • 1 egg (yolk kept for topping)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 4–4½ cups flour
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 500g beef mince
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp molasses
  • 2 large onions
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • burger cheese slices
  • roasted red pepper
  • egg yolk
  • sesame seeds
  • 4 tbsp mayo
  • 2 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tbsp mustard
  • 2 finely chopped gherkins

Method

  1. Mix milk, water, sugar & yeast; rest 5 min.
  2. Add egg white, salt & flour; knead to a soft dough, add butter and knead 5 more min.
  3. Cover and rise ~1 hr.
  4. Cook sliced onions slowly in oil with salt & sugar ~15 min till caramelised.
  5. Mix mince with salt, pepper & molasses and shape patties.
  6. Split dough into tangerine-sized balls, flatten, fill with a patty, onions, pepper & cheese and seal.
  7. Seam-side down on a tray, brush with yolk, add sesame.
  8. Bake 180°C for 20–25 min till golden.
