Stuffed jacket potato with garlic prawns

Ingredients

  • 1 large potato
  • 1 tsp butter
  • handful grated mozzarella
  • garlic prawns (gambas al ajillo) with guindilla chilli
  • olive oil

Method

  1. Wrap the potato in foil and air fry until completely tender.
  2. Unwrap, halve, add butter and mozzarella and mash the flesh with a fork until creamy.
  3. Top with the garlic prawns and chilli.
