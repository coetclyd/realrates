Baked garlic butter chicken shells with three-cheese sauce

Ingredients

  • 450g (1 lb) chicken breast in thick strips
  • 340g (12 oz) pasta shells
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 1½ cups double cream
  • 1¼ cups grated mozzarella
  • 1 cup grated white cheddar
  • ¾ cup parmesan
