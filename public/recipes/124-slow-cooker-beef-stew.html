Slow cooker beef stew

Ingredients

  • 600g beef in small chunks
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 2–3 carrots
  • 500g new potatoes (halved)
  • 2 celery sticks
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tbsp garlic paste
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 700ml beef stock (2 cubes/stock pot)
  • splash of Worcestershire sauce
  • splash of soy sauce
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • chopped rosemary
  • salt

Method

  1. Toss beef with cornflour, onion, carrots and celery in the slow cooker, then add everything else.
  2. Cook on high 5 hrs or low 7–8 hrs.
  3. Thicken with a spoon of gravy granules if needed.
  4. Serve with crusty bread and butter.
