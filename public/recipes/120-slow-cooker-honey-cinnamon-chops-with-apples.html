Slow cooker honey cinnamon chops with apples

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in pork chops
  • 2 red apples, cored and sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp light brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 120ml apple juice (or chicken stock)
  • 1 tbsp cornflour mixed with 2 tbsp cold water

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a frying pan and brown the chops for 2–3 min each side.
  2. Put the onion and half the apple slices in the slow cooker, sit the chops on top and add the rest of the apples.
  3. Mix the honey, sugar, cinnamon and apple juice, and pour it over.
  4. Cook on low for 6 hours or high for 3–4 hours until the pork is tender.
  5. Lift out the chops and apples and keep warm.
  6. Stir the cornflour mix into the juices, turn to high and cook 15 min until thickened.
  7. Pour the sauce over the chops and serve with mash and greens.
