Tortilla "boat" with tomato, beef bacon, cheese & egg

Ingredients

  • tortilla
  • cottage cheese (or grated cheese) for the edges
  • tomato sauce
  • beef bacon/ham
  • grated cheese
  • 1 egg
  • egg yolk for brushing

Method

  1. Spread cheese round the edge of the tortilla and roll the edges in to make a boat shape (see video).
  2. Fill the middle with tomato sauce, bacon/ham and cheese, crack in an egg.
  3. Brush edges with yolk and bake at 220°C for 10 min.
