Potato-crust bacon & cheese quiche

Ingredients

  • potatoes (russet/Yukon Gold)
  • oil
  • salt
  • 6 eggs
  • ½ cup ricotta
  • ½ cup grated Colby Jack (or mild cheddar) plus extra
  • ¼ cup diced spring onions plus extra
  • 3–4 slices thick bacon (cooked, chopped) plus extra
  • sour cream/crème fraîche
  • hot honey
  • chives

Method

  1. Boil halved potatoes until tender.
  2. Grease a springform tin, add potatoes and press into a crust up the sides with an oiled glass.
  3. Spray with oil, salt well, bake 220°C (425°F) 20–25 min.
  4. Whisk eggs, ricotta, cheese, spring onions & bacon; pour into crust, top with more cheese.
  5. Bake 180°C (350°F) 40–45 min until set.
  6. Cool a few mins, unmould, top with flaky salt, bacon & spring onions.
