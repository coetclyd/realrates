Potato & cheese omelette (folded)

Ingredients

  • 1 potato
  • 1 egg
  • 100g mozzarella
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil

Method

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Peel the potato into thin ribbons with a veg peeler and spread in a thin layer over the base of the pan; fry until golden.
  3. Pour over the beaten, salted egg and cook until just set.
  4. Flip and brown the other side.
  5. Sprinkle cheese over one half, fold over and cook until melted.
  6. Serve hot.
