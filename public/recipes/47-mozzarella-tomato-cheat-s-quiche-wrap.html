Mozzarella & tomato "cheat's quiche" wrap

Ingredients

  • 1 high-protein wrap
  • 100g cherry tomatoes (sliced)
  • 100g mozzarella (chopped)
  • 2 medium eggs (whisked)
  • sriracha (optional)
  • basil

Method

  1. Press the wrap into an ovenproof dish, add tomatoes and mozzarella, pour over the whisked eggs.
  2. Bake 15 min at 180°C.
  3. Add basil at the end.
