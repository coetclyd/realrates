Crispy grated potato, egg & cheese breakfast

Ingredients

  • 1 potato
  • 1 egg
  • kashar (or mozzarella) cheese
  • salt
  • oil

Method

  1. Grate the potato, squeeze out the liquid and salt it.
  2. Fry in a greased pan on both sides until golden.
  3. Add the egg and cheese on top and cook on low heat until set.
