Potato & egg skillet omelette

Ingredients

  • 1 thinly sliced potato
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 3 eggs
  • grated cheese
  • tomato
  • parsley
  • salt
  • black pepper
  • chilli flakes

Method

  1. Cook the thin potato slices slowly in the oil.
  2. Pour over beaten eggs, turn heat to lowest, add grated cheese, cover until melted.
  3. Top with thinly sliced tomato and parsley, finish cooking and serve.
