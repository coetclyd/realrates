Stuffed breakfast baguette

Ingredients

  • 1 baguette
  • eggs
  • grated cheese
  • sausage or ham
  • pepper (bell)
  • fresh herbs

Method

  1. Cut the baguette into sections and hollow out the middles.
  2. Mix eggs with chopped sausage/ham, pepper, herbs and seasoning.
  3. Fill the bread cups, top with cheese and bake until the bread is crisp, the egg is set and the cheese golden.
