Air fryer parmesan courgette fries

Ingredients

  • 1½ tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • ½ cup finely grated parmesan
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • ½ tsp sea salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • parsley
  • lemon
  • garlic aioli or marinara

Method

  1. Cut courgettes into thick wedges and pat very dry.
  2. Toss in oil.
  3. Mix parmesan and seasonings, sprinkle over and press onto the cut sides.
  4. Air fry at 200°C, skin-side down in a single layer, for 8–10 min until golden (no need to flip).
  5. Garnish and serve.
