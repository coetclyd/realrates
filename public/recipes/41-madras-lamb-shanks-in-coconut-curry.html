Madras lamb shanks in coconut curry

Ingredients

  • 4 lamb shanks
  • 2 tbsp veg oil
  • 1 large onion
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 tbsp Madras curry powder
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 2 tsp coriander
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • 4 cardamom pods
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • 2 tbsp tomato purée
  • 400g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 400ml tin full-fat coconut milk
  • 2 cups (~475ml) beef or lamb stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp coriander leaves
  • fresh chilli (optional)
  • Serve with rice
  • naan or roti

Method

  1. Season and brown shanks in oil in a casserole 8–10 min; remove.
  2. Soften onion 6–8 min, add garlic & ginger 1 min, then all the spices 1 min, then tomato purée 1 min.
  3. Add tomatoes, coconut milk, stock, sugar & bay leaves.
  4. Return shanks, cover and simmer very low 3–3½ hrs (or oven 160°C) until falling apart.
  5. Remove shanks, reduce sauce 10–15 min if needed, stir in lemon juice, return shanks and top with coriander/chilli.
