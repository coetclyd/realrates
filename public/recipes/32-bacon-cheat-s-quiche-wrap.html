Bacon "cheat's quiche" wrap

Ingredients

  • 1 high-protein wrap
  • 3 rashers back bacon
  • 3 medium eggs

Method

  1. Press the wrap into an ovenproof dish/tin to make a case, add bacon and crack in the eggs; bake at 180°C for 15 min.
  2. 500 kcal, 46g protein.)
