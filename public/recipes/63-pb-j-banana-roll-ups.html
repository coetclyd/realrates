PB&J banana roll-ups

Ingredients

  • tortilla
  • peanut butter (or other nut/seed butter)
  • raspberry jam
  • hemp seeds
  • banana

Method

  1. Warm the tortilla, spread with peanut butter and jam, sprinkle hemp seeds, roll up around a banana, slice and serve with extra jam for dipping.
