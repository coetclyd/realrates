One-pan pork dumpling lasagne

Ingredients

  • 350g pork mince
  • 2 spring onions
  • 100g Chinese chives/leek
  • 1 tsp chicken stock concentrate in 60ml water
  • 3 tbsp soy
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1–2 tbsp neutral oil
  • ~1 pack dumpling wrappers
  • 60ml water + ½ tsp stock concentrate
  • 1 tbsp soy
  • 2 tbsp chilli oil
  • ½ tsp sesame seeds
  • Spring onions to garnish

Method

  1. Mix all filling ingredients vigorously 2–3 min until sticky.
  2. Oil a large non-stick pan; layer wrappers and filling like a lasagne.
  3. Pour the stock-water round the pan, cover and cook on medium ~8 min until pork is cooked.
  4. Uncover and cook 2–4 min more to crisp the bottom.
  5. Drizzle with the chilli sauce and top with spring onions.
