Chicken & courgette casserole

Ingredients

  • 2 courgettes
  • 1 chicken breast
  • 4 eggs
  • 100g sour cream
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tomato
  • 100g hard cheese (e.g. cheddar)
  • 50g parmesan
  • salt
  • pepper
  • favourite spices

Method

  1. Grate courgettes, salt lightly, rest 5–10 min and squeeze out liquid.
  2. Dice chicken; finely chop onion; slice tomato.
  3. Mix eggs and sour cream, season; stir in courgette and grated cheese.
  4. Put half the courgette mix in a baking dish, top with chicken and onion, cover with the rest.
  5. Top with tomato and parmesan.
  6. Cover with foil, bake 30 min at 180°C, then uncover for 10 min more.
  7. Rest 5–10 min before cutting.
