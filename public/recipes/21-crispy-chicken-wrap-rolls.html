Crispy chicken wrap rolls

Ingredients

  • 8 tortillas
  • 1kg chicken breast
  • ~50ml vegetable oil
  • 1 onion
  • 150g cherry tomatoes (halved)
  • 1 green pepper
  • 200ml cream (optional)
  • curry powder
  • 250g grated cheese (kasar or mozzarella/cheddar)
  • Coating: 1 beaten egg
  • ~2 cups lightly crushed cornflakes

Method

  1. Dice chicken and fry on high heat in the oil.
  2. Add the chopped veg and fry again.
  3. Stir in cream and spices, then take off the heat.
  4. Fill and roll the tortillas, brush with egg, coat in cornflakes and bake at 170°C until golden and the cheese has melted.
