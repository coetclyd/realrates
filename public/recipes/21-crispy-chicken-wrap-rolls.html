Crispy chicken wrap rolls

Ingredients

  • 8 tortillas
  • 1kg chicken breast
  • ~50ml vegetable oil
  • 1 onion
  • 1 red pepper
  • 1 green pepper
  • 200ml cream (optional)
  • black pepper
  • curry powder
  • salt
  • 250g grated cheese (kasar or mozzarella/cheddar)
  • Coating: 1 beaten egg
  • ~2 cups lightly crushed cornflakes
