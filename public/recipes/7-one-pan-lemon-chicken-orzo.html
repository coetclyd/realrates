One-pan lemon chicken orzo

Ingredients

  • 1 cup orzo
  • 1¾ cups chicken broth
  • ½ cup double cream
  • 3–4 chicken breasts
  • 2 cups spinach
  • ½ cup parmesan
  • 1 cup grated mozzarella
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • ½ tsp each salt
  • pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
