One-pan lemon chicken orzo

Ingredients

  • 1 cup orzo
  • 1¾ cups chicken broth
  • ½ cup double cream
  • 3–4 chicken breasts
  • 2 cups spinach
  • ½ cup parmesan
  • 1 cup grated mozzarella
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • ½ tsp each salt
  • pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Method

  1. Oven 400°F (200°C).
  2. In a 9x13 dish mix orzo, broth, cream, garlic, lemon juice & seasonings.
  3. Add spinach, chicken on top, drizzle oil.
  4. Cover tightly with foil, bake 30 min.
  5. Remove foil, add parmesan & mozzarella and bake until melted/golden.
