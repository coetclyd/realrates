Loaded chicken, courgette & squash bake

Ingredients

  • 2 courgettes
  • 2 yellow squash (or more courgette)
  • 6 rashers bacon (cooked, crumbled)
  • 1½ cups grated cheddar
  • ½ cup grated mozzarella
  • ½ cup sour cream
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp chopped spring onions

Method

  1. Oven 190°C; grease a 9x13in dish.
  2. Toss sliced veg with oil, garlic and spices, then toss chicken in.
  3. Spread in the dish, cover with foil and bake 20 min.
  4. Uncover, spread sour cream over, top with cheeses and bacon.
  5. Bake uncovered 15–20 min until chicken is cooked and cheese bubbling.
  6. Top with spring onions; rest 5 min.
